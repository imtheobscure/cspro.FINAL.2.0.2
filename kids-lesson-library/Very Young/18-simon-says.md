# 18 · Simon Says 🤸

**Band:** Very Young (2–5) · **Type:** Game (screen + room) · **Time:** 3–5 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
The classic command game, run from the screen: "Simon says jump!" — and when Simon *doesn't* say
it, the whole room stands still and giggles. It is TPR with a listening trap on top: the child
must understand the command **and** catch the magic words. For 2–4 the trap is removed (every
command counts); from 4 the trap appears, gently, with the child winning most of the time.
(Classroom classic; ego-preserving because "losing" is just standing still.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **understands and performs 6+ commands** without translation.
- The child **listens for the key words** ("Simon says") — a real attention skill.
- The room gets its movement; nobody ever sits out.

## 3 · How a round runs
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. A huge command card appears: emoji + the spoken line.
3. The room does it; the screen confirms with a bounce and a comment.
4. From the second lap: sometimes the words "Simon says" are missing — a "FREEZE!" card.
5. Every child stays in; the freeze is the joke, not a punishment.
6. Finish: "Simon says well done!" with each child's name where the host provides them.

## 4 · Tutor & room notes
- Under 4: turn the trap off entirely — every command is a Simon command.
- One instruction per card; never two-step directions.
- Model the commands yourself; exaggerate the freeze into stillness.
- Safety: small space, nothing to knock over, check the floor.
