# 03 · Move & Do (TPR)

**Band:** Very Young (2–5) · **Type:** Lesson (movement / TPR) · **Time:** 5–7 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
Six to eight physical commands — jump, clap, sit down, touch your nose, give me the ball — taught
by doing, never by explaining. Comprehension is proved with the body, so a pre-verbal child can
succeed completely; movement also buys the session its energy release. (Total Physical Response,
Asher; used by every major young-learner programme.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **understands** 5+ commands without translation, proved by doing.
- The child learns **one new action word** mixed among known ones.
- The child **gives a command back** (the reversal moment — highest-value turn in the game).
- The tutor sees instantly which commands are still not understood.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Teach** — one command per screen: big emoji, the line spoken, the action demonstrated.
3. **Faster & slower** — same commands, playful speed changes; the child copies.
4. **Your turn** — the child picks a command card for the grown-up to obey.
5. Finish: "You moved like a frog!" + one command for home.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- One instruction per screen, one action — never two-step ("touch your nose and jump" is overload).
- Never repeat a command because the child "should" know it; re-model and move on.
- Award nothing, score nothing; the doing is the reward.
- Safety: check the floor; nothing to climb; keep it to a small room.
