# 11 · Listen & Point 🎮

**Band:** Very Young (2–4) · **Type:** Game (screen) · **Time:** 3–4 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
The child hears a word and points at the matching picture among three. Comprehension is years
ahead of production at this age, so pointing lets the child succeed completely without saying a
word; hearing the word first trains the listening ear reading will later lean on. This is the
"listen and choose" engine, sized for a room. (Lingokids listen-and-choose; Cambridge pre-A1
picture tasks.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **picks the right picture** for 4–6 spoken words.
- The child **hears each word 3+ times** across the game.
- The tutor sees which words are solid without any testing feel.

## 3 · How a round runs
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. A round appears: **one spoken word** + 3 giant pictures (one correct, two unmistakable decoys).
3. The child points/taps; correct blossoms with the round's own sound; wrong wobbles + "nearly".
4. Read-it-again button on every round — with a slower repeat.
5. After 4 rounds: a parade of everything the child found + "You found them all!".
6. The tutor can replay with the same words or press "new words" for a second lap.

## 4 · Tutor & room notes
- Read the word with the child if they freeze; pointing alone is the answer.
- Options must be visually unmistakable (different animal, different colour) — never similar.
- The correct picture must never sit in the same position twice in a row.
- No score, no timer, no elimination. Wrong taps stay tappable.
