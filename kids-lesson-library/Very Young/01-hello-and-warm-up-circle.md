# 01 · Hello & Warm-Up Circle

**Band:** Very Young (2–5) · **Type:** Lesson (opening ritual) · **Time:** 3–5 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
The same short ritual that opens **every** session: a hello song with the child's name in it, a
feelings check with big faces, and one wake-up movement. Predictability is the pedagogy at this
age — the same order tells a two-year-old "English time has started" before a single new word
is taught. (Twinkl, Lingokids and preschool programmes all open with a routine song.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **greets and is greeted**: waves, says "hello", hears their own name in English.
- The child **shows a feeling** by tapping a face (happy, sleepy, sad, excited) — no words required.
- The child **moves once** (stomp, clap, stretch) to arrive in the room.
- The tutor gets a 30-second read of the child's mood and energy for the session.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Hello song** — 4 short lines, one action each; the child's name is sung twice.
3. **How are you?** — four giant feeling faces; tap to hear "happy!", face animates.
4. **Wake up!** — one movement screen: stomp 3×, clap 3×, reach up high.
5. **Goodbye preview** — "Next we'll play with a duck!" (teases the session), finish chime.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- The child may only watch for weeks; joining in is the win, not singing on pitch.
- Sing yourself — the on-screen voice is a model, your voice is the lesson.
- If the child taps every face, name each one warmly and move on; never correct.
- No reading anywhere; the feelings screen is pictures only.
