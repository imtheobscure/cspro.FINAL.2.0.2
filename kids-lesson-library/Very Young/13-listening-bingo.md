# 13 · Listening Bingo 🎧

**Band:** Very Young (3–5) · **Type:** Game (screen) · **Time:** 4–5 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
A 3×3 board of pictures; the voice calls a word; the child stamps the picture they heard. Hearing
a word inside a stream of sound — not told in advance — is what phonemic awareness is built on:
the child holds the sound in working memory and matches it to a picture, with no printed hint.
The board is pictures, so nothing is read. (Bingo from every ESL young-learner toolbox.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **recognises 5+ spoken words** with no printed text.
- The child **holds a sound in memory** long enough to scan nine pictures — the real skill.
- The whole class (or the pair) plays at once; one board, many turns.

## 3 · How a round runs
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. A 3×3 board of nine emoji pictures appears; the voice calls one word, slowly.
3. The child taps the picture; correct = a stamp lands with a ding; wrong = wobble + replay.
4. "Say it again" is a right, not a penalty — every call has a slow repeat.
5. Five calls make a line, a full house, or just five stamps — all count as winning.
6. Finish: the board turns into a rainbow + "BINGO ears!".

## 4 · Tutor & room notes
- No two board pictures may start with the same sound (or the game becomes guessing).
- The tutor may call in their own voice for a harder round — the button is there.
- Replay the same board with a different word order; familiarity is the point.
- No timer; a slow room is a successful room.
