# 06 · Sensory & Pretend Play

**Band:** Very Young (2–5) · **Type:** Lesson (sensory play / pretend) · **Time:** 5–8 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
A pretend scene the adult and child act out with **real things** — feeding the pet, washing the
duck, hiding teddy, a tea party — while the screen holds the scene, the words and the timing.
Language rides on the hands: children this age learn best when a word is attached to something
they can touch. (Sensory-play practice from Montessori/Reggio; Brightwheel sensory guides.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **acts out 4–5 English words** with objects (give, eat, wash, hide, sleep).
- The child **hears one new word 5+ times** inside the pretend frame.
- The child experiences English as something you **do together**, not a test.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!" with the scene's hero (teddy/duck/pet).
2. **Set the scene** — the picture + the props list for the tutor (a bowl, a cloth, a toy).
3. **Play beats** — 4–5 beats, each: one spoken line + one action + the object to use.
4. **Swap roles** — the child gives the adult one instruction (their choice of two cards).
5. **Tidy-up song** — two lines that turn putting things away into the ending.
6. Finish: the pet "says thank you" + the family line.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- Every activity must be safe to mouth and easy to wash — this age explores by tasting.
- If the child's play drifts, follow their lead and name what they are doing in English.
- The screen stays on the scene; do not switch slides mid-play.
- Two choices maximum whenever the child chooses.
