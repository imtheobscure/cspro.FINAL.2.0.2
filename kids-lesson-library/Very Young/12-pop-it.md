# 12 · Pop It! 🎮

**Band:** Very Young (2–4) · **Type:** Game (screen) · **Time:** 3–4 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
Balloons drift up carrying pictures; the child pops the one the voice names. Contingency
learning — "I do something and the world answers" — is the engine of toddler development, and a
pop is the purest version: instant, colourful, caused entirely by the child. A wrong tap only
wobbles; nothing is ever taken away. (Cause-and-effect apps in every 2-year-old product.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **taps the target picture** among 4 drifting balloons.
- The child hears the target word **the moment the round starts** and on every replay.
- Cause-and-effect play keeps the child **engaged and moving** — the session's fun beat.

## 3 · How a round runs
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. Balloons rise slowly with 4 pictures; the voice says "Pop the duck!".
3. Correct pop: confetti burst + the word celebrated + a count of pops.
4. Wrong tap: the balloon wobbles, a soft sound, "not that one — look for the duck 🦆".
5. 3 rounds, then a final burst with every popped balloon.
6. Replay with the same word (recommended) or one new word.

## 4 · Tutor & room notes
- Balloon speed is slow on purpose; a 2-year-old needs time to aim.
- Never remove a balloon for a wrong tap — the target stays reachable.
- If the child just pops everything, name each picture warmly; the game forgives.
- Sound is short and soft; no sudden loud pop.
