# 15 · Where Is It? 🙈

**Band:** Very Young (2–3) · **Type:** Game (screen) · **Time:** 3–4 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
A picture hides behind one of two giant cards; the child finds it. Object permanence — knowing
things still exist when hidden — is the cognitive milestone of this exact age, so the game sits
precisely on the child's developmental edge, and it carries receptive vocabulary for free: they
are looking for the *duck*, not for just anything. (Peekaboo, the original toddler game.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **finds 3 hidden pictures** on request.
- The child hears **one word 6+ times** (call, hunt, celebration).
- Cause-and-effect joy keeps the child attending — the session's calm, fun beat.

## 3 · How a round runs
1. Splash → "Let's go! Where is the duck?".
2. Two giant cards; a "hidden" wobble passes behind one; both settle.
3. The child taps the card they think hides the picture.
4. Correct: the card flips, the picture pops out, confetti + "Found it! Quack quack!".
5. Wrong: the other card gently peeks; "not yet — look again 💛"; keep tapping.
6. 3 rounds → all finds line up + "You found everything!".

## 4 · Tutor & room notes
- Exactly **two** choices — three is overload before four.
- The correct side never repeats more than once in a row.
- If the child simply taps both, that is the game working; celebrate the find.
- Hide the picture slowly enough to follow with the eyes.
