# 16 · Sing & Do 🎵

**Band:** Very Young (2–5) · **Type:** Game (screen, karaoke) · **Time:** 3–4 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
A four-line song on screen, one giant action per line, sung twice: once to listen, once together.
Melody plus movement stores each word twice — as sound and as body — which is why a chant survives
to next week and a spoken list does not. The second run changes **one** word so the child hears
the pattern flex. (Super Simple Songs karaoke format.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **does the action** for all four lines.
- The child **joins one sound or word** per line.
- The target word is heard **8+ times** with a tiny variation, all inside a melody.

## 3 · How a round runs
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Listen run** — the song plays with bouncing pictures, no words to read.
3. **Together run** — line by line; big replay; the action demonstrated on screen.
4. **Spot the change** — one word changes (duck → frog); the child gasps and points.
5. **Freeze** — music pauses; everyone freezes mid-action; giggle; continue.
6. Finish: "You sang the duck song!" + the family line.

## 4 · Tutor & room notes
- 1400–1800 ms per line; the on-screen pace should feel almost slow.
- Every line must be singable by an untrained adult.
- The child may just bounce; participation is the win.
- End with the family line so the song goes home.
