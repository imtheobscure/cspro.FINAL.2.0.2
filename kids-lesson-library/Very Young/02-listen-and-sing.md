# 02 · Listen & Sing

**Band:** Very Young (2–5) · **Type:** Lesson (song & chant) · **Time:** 5–8 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
A tiny song or chant of 4 short lines built on **one** target word, with an action for every
line, played twice: once to listen, once to do, then a second verse with exactly one word
changed. Melody and movement store the word twice (sound + body) — the reason a chant survives
to next week when a spoken list does not. (Super Simple Songs, Lingokids, Cocomelon method.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **joins in** on at least one sound or word per line ("quack", "again").
- The child **does the action** for each line (waddle, splash, wave).
- The target word is heard **5+ times** inside a melody, with a tiny variation in verse 2.
- The tutor leaves with a song the family can repeat at bath time (caregiver note).

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Listen** — the whole song plays, one picture per line, no words on screen.
3. **Do it** — line by line: hear the line, see the action, do it together (big replay button).
4. **Sing together** — the song again with a bounce prompt on each beat.
5. **Verse 2** — one word changes (duck → frog); the child spots it with a giggle.
6. Finish: "You sang the duck song!" + the family line.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- Pace 1400–1800 ms per line; slower is always better than faster.
- If the child freezes, sing it yourself and let them watch; play it again tomorrow.
- Do not ask for words — sounds count ("quack quack" is a full answer).
- The family note: one line to sing in the bath, no device.
