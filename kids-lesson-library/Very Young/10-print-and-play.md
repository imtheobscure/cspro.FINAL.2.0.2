# 10 · Print & Play (Worksheet, No Text)

**Band:** Very Young (2–5) · **Type:** Activity (printable) · **Time:** 5 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file with print stylesheet

## 1 · What it is
A one-page activity the adult prints: big shapes to colour, 3 items to match by line, 2 pictures
to cut and stick, one hand-print or finger-paint prompt. **No text instructions for the child** —
the adult reads them from the on-screen version. Every item must be doable with a thick crayon,
standing at a table. (Pre-writing practice from preschool curricula; ABCmouse printable line.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child practises **grip, colouring, line-matching and cutting** (fine motor, age-legal).
- The child hears the target words **while the hands work** — the adult narrates.
- The tutor gets a **reusable printout** for the week, with an adult instruction line per item.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!" — the activity opens on the screen version.
2. **Colour** — one huge shape/animal to colour; the tutor reads "colour the duck yellow".
3. **Match** — three items to a line-match (duck→pond, bear→cave, cat→bed).
4. **Cut & stick** — two big pictures with thick outlines and a safe-scissors note.
5. **Fingerprints** — one paint prompt that becomes a picture when pressed.
6. **Print** — one button prints the A4 page; on the back: the word list for the fridge.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- Say the word **while the hand moves**; do not test it.
- Safe scissors only, and "with a grown-up" printed where the cut is.
- If the child scribbles across the whole page, that IS the activity — praise the motion.
- Print in greyscale too; not every home has a colour printer.
