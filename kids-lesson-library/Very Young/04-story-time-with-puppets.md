# 04 · Story Time with Puppets

**Band:** Very Young (2–5) · **Type:** Lesson (story / listening) · **Time:** 5–8 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
A four-picture story of three to five sentences, told aloud by the adult with a puppet (or a
sock) acting it. The child joins in on one repeated sound per page ("quack!", "oh no!") and one
action. Stories give the session its emotional hook; puppets give shy children someone else to
talk to. (Khan Academy Kids interactive books; classic preschool story-time practice.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **listens to English for 2+ minutes** without needing translation.
- The child **makes one repeated sound or gesture** at the right moment of the story.
- The child **answers one picture question** (Who is this? Where is the duck?) by pointing.
- The child meets the target words **inside a story**, not a list.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Meet the puppet** — it says hello, the child waves back.
3. **Story, page by page** — 1 sentence per page, one big picture, one join-in sound marked.
4. **Join-in again** — the story replayed with the child's sound/action spotlighted.
5. **One question per page** — point to answer; correct picture blooms.
6. Finish: the puppet says "Again tomorrow?" + the family line.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- Tell it slowly with a different voice per character; the screen is scenery, not the narrator.
- If the child wants the same page five times, that is the lesson working — replay it.
- No reading; the one question is answered by pointing.
- Keep the puppet/prop visible for the whole story.
