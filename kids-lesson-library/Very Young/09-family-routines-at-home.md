# 09 · Family Routines at Home

**Band:** Very Young (2–5) · **Type:** Lesson (parent coaching + home missions) · **Time:** 5 min in class, all week at home · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
One daily routine — bath, breakfast, dressing or bedtime — with the 2–3 things that happen in
it, the exact English a parent says, and a chant that makes it fun. The class time is spent
teaching **the grown-up**, not the child: at this age, minutes at home beat minutes on a screen.
(Twinkl parent guides; the "parent as co-teacher" model in every young-learner programme.)

## 2 · Goals
- The parent can say **5 English phrases** in the routine without translating.
- The child hears the same 5 phrases **every day for a week** — the actual learning.
- One chant turns a chore (bath, teeth) into a game both of them enjoy.
- The tutor gets a written plan plus a printable card for the fridge.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Pick the routine** — four icons (bath, breakfast, dressing, bedtime).
3. **The steps** — 3 moments, each with the parent's exact English line + a pronunciation hint.
4. **The chant** — 4 lines to sing during the routine; play once, hum it together.
5. **The fridge card** — a printable summary with the 5 phrases and the chant.
6. Finish: "One routine, one week!" + a tiny tracker (7 dots to colour).

## 4 · Tutor notes
- Praise the parent's effort in class; they are the learner here.
- Phrase count is capped at 5 — more will not be used.
- Say one thing NOT to do: no translating word-for-word, no quizzing the child.
- Print the fridge card in class so it actually lands on the fridge.
