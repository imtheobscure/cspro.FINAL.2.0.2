# 08 · Feelings & Faces

**Band:** Very Young (2–5) · **Type:** Lesson (social-emotional learning) · **Time:** 4–6 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
Four faces — happy, sad, sleepy, angry — with the English word, a sound and a tiny calming
action for each. The child names how **they** feel and how the **character** feels. SEL is a
first-class subject in early learning (Khan Academy Kids' whole "social-emotional" strand), and
for a small child the feeling words are the most useful English they will ever own.

## 2 · Goals
- The child **recognises and names** 3+ feelings, by tapping and/or saying.
- The child **matches a feeling to a situation** ("teddy lost his ball — how does he feel?").
- The child learns **one calming action** (breath, hug, squeeze) tied to a word.
- Emotional vocabulary becomes usable at home (family note).

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Meet the faces** — each face speaks its word; the child copies the face (mirror play).
3. **How do you feel?** — the child taps their own face; the adult names it warmly.
4. **Story feelings** — 3 tiny situations with the question "how does he feel?" and 2 faces.
5. **Calm-down** — one breath/hug action with the word ("slow… breathe…").
6. Finish: "You know four feelings!" + the family line.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- Never correct a feeling; any answer is "yes, and…". This lesson is safety, not vocabulary.
- If the child's own answer is sad or angry, stop the quiz and stay with them.
- Use the same four faces everywhere in the app so the vocabulary compounds.
- Keep the calming action genuinely slow — model the pace.
