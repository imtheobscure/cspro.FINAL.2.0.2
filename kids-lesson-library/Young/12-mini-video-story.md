# 12 · Mini Video Story

**Band:** Young (5–12) · **Type:** Lesson (video + retell) · **Time:** 20–30 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
A 60-second animated story in 6–8 shots, built from emoji and CSS (no network video), with the
on-screen text kept to four words per shot, a narrator line per shot, and three questions to ask
straight after watching. Two shots are marked "say it with me" so the child talks during the
video, not only after. Watching plus retelling is one of the strongest input→output sequences in
early language learning. (Lingokids video-story format; Khan Kids storybooks.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **watches a 60-second story in English** and follows the plot.
- The child **repeats 2+ lines** during the "say it with me" shots.
- The child **answers 3 questions and retells** 3 sentences in order.
- The target words appear **inside the narration**, not as a list.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Watch once** — the whole story, no interruptions, no text to read.
3. **Watch again** — pauses at the two "say it with me" shots; the child repeats.
4. **Order the shots** — drag three shots back into order.
5. **Three questions** — one at a time, full-sentence answers modelled.
6. **Retell** — the child narrates over the still shots (their own words encouraged).
7. **Wrap** — "You told the story!" + the family line.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- Four words maximum of on-screen text per shot; the narration carries the rest.
- The first watch is uninterrupted — do not pause to translate.
- Retell in the child's own words; perfection is not the target, sequence is.
- If the child is shy, they can order shots and point while the tutor narrates.
