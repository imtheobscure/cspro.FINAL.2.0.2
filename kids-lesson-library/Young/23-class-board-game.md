# 23 · Class Board Game 🎲

**Band:** Young (6–12) · **Type:** Game (screen, spinner) · **Time:** 10–12 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
A wheel of six tasks spins and lands on one — say a sentence with "duck", spell "pond", act out
"swim", name three animals with wings. The wheel removes the tutor from the role of judge:
nobody loses face when the spinner decides, and turn-taking — a skill this age is still learning
by doing — gets practised every spin. Each segment reuses the lesson's language in a different
mode, which is spaced retrieval with a game wrapped around it. (Spin-the-wheel speaking games.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **completes 4+ tasks** across speaking, spelling and meaning.
- The class **takes turns fairly** — the wheel, not the tutor, decides who.
- The lesson's language is **reused in three modes** (say, spell, act).
- The tutor gets one strong skill and one weak skill per spin, privately.

## 3 · How a round runs
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. Enter or confirm team names (or one child + the tutor as a team).
3. **Spin** — the wheel animates dramatically, lands with a ding.
4. The segment's task is shown big; every task is under 30 seconds, zero preparation.
5. The team does it; the tutor taps a private ✓ or "again later"; a star for the class.
6. After each full rotation: teams swap; 6–12 spins per game; end with a joint celebration.

## 4 · Tutor & room notes
- Six segments, each a different mode so one strong skill cannot carry the team.
- Tasks must be doable in 30 seconds with no props.
- The private ✓ list is for tomorrow's warm-up, not today's ranking.
- For a single child, they play against their own last spin — beat your own record.
