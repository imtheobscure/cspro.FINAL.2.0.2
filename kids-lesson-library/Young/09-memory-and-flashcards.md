# 09 · Memory & Flashcards

**Band:** Young (5–12) · **Type:** Lesson (spaced retrieval) · **Time:** 15–25 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
A deck of six to eight cards: the front is the word (with an emoji cue), the back is the meaning
or a first-language gloss. The child says the answer **before** turning the card, then marks
"I know it" — and that judgement is metacognition, the skill that makes a learner study what
they do not yet know. Every fifth card mixes two earlier words. Wrong cards return before the
deck ends. (Leitner boxes; Anki-style spaced retrieval for kids.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **recalls 6+ words** from the front before the flip.
- The child **self-assesses honestly** ("I know it" / "not yet") at least twice.
- Two earlier words **return inside the deck** (spacing, not massing).
- The tutor leaves with a **map of known vs unknown** cards.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Deck** — front shows the word + emoji; the child says it or its meaning aloud.
3. **Flip** — the answer appears; the child presses "I knew it" or "not yet".
4. **Review cards** — every fifth card mixes two earlier words ("say both").
5. **Second lap** — only the "not yet" cards return, shuffled.
6. **Wrap** — "You mastered X cards!" + a 3-card deck for home + family note.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- The mark is the child's call; never override it — honest self-assessment is the skill.
- A card that fails twice gets parked for next session, not drilled now.
- The home deck is the three cards the child nearly knew, not the ten they already do.
- Meaning in the child's first language is allowed when it helps.
