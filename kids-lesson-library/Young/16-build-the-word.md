# 16 · Build the Word 🐝

**Band:** Young (5–8) · **Type:** Game (screen, spelling) · **Time:** 6–9 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
The child hears a word and taps the letter tiles in order to build it — phonics in action, and far
stronger than recognising a word from a list. Tiles that can be placed wrongly, with an instant
wobble rather than a mark, let the child self-correct without the sting of being wrong in front
of anyone. Words share a pattern (word family, vowel sound or prefix) so the child learns a rule,
not four random spellings. (Encoding/spelling bee; DfE phonics check style.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **spells 4–6 pattern-sharing words** from hearing them.
- The child **self-corrects** at least once using the tiles (not the tutor's help).
- The tutor sees which **letter patterns are still hard**.

## 3 · How a round runs
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Listen** — the word is spoken clearly, once slowly; a picture cue appears.
3. **Build** — tap tiles in order; they snap into slots; a wrong tile wobbles and returns.
4. **Check** — the completed word is spoken back; correct = a bee flies a loop + a star.
5. **Harder lap** — one new word of the same pattern, plus 2 distractor letters on the tray.
6. Finish: every word read aloud one last time + "Perfect spelling!" (or "Nearly all!").

## 4 · Tutor & room notes
- Shortest words first; same pattern throughout.
- No timer by default; add one only for a child who loves speed.
- The wobble is the correction — resist spelling it for the child.
- Magnetic letters or paper tiles are the no-screen twin.
