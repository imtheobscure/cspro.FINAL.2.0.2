# 13 · Family & Tutor Guide

**Band:** Young (5–12) · **Type:** Lesson (teacher & family) · **Time:** 5 min to read in class · **Runtime:** one HTML file with print stylesheet

## 1 · What it is
One printable page for the grown-ups: what the child is learning this week, three 10-minute
activities, one thing to listen for, and how to praise effort rather than correctness. Family
involvement is one of the strongest predictors of language progress; the guide's job is to make
it three easy things, not homework for the parent. (Parent-engagement practice; Twinkl home
guides.)

## 2 · Goals
- The family can **run 3 ten-minute activities** without preparation.
- The family knows the **one thing to listen for** this week.
- Praise is effort-based at home too (the same language the tutor uses).
- The tutor gets a **one-page summary** to send or print.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **This week** — the goal in one sentence (child-facing words in bold).
3. **Three activities** — car game, kitchen game, bedtime game; each with the exact English lines.
4. **Listen for** — the one sound/word/structure to notice and celebrate.
5. **How to praise** — two example phrases, one thing not to do (no quizzing, no translating).
6. **Print** — A4, one page, fridge-ready; optionally a voice-note version for the tutor.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- Three activities maximum; a fourth never happens.
- Write the child's actual words in bold so the parent knows exactly what to say.
- "One thing not to do" prevents the most common home error.
- Print it in class — the fridge beats the inbox.
