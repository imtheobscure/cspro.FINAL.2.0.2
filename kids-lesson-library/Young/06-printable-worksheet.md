# 06 · Printable Worksheet

**Band:** Young (5–12) · **Type:** Activity (printable) · **Time:** 15–20 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file with print stylesheet

## 1 · What it is
A one-page sheet in three graded parts: (1) match or circle, (2) write or complete the word in a
ruled box, (3) finish two sentences about the child's **own life**. Deliberately graded —
recognise → recall → produce — so the child starts with a guaranteed success and ends writing
about themselves. An answer key goes on the back for the adult. (English workbook progression in
ABCmouse/Twinkl packs.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **completes a recognition task** with no writing.
- The child **writes or completes 4–6 target words** in ruled boxes.
- The child **writes two personal sentences** using the target language.
- The tutor gets a marked artefact for the learner's folder + an answer key.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!" (screen version with the same three parts).
2. **Part 1 · Match/Circle** — the child taps on screen or on paper; instant praise.
3. **Part 2 · Complete** — letter boxes with the first letter given; keyboard or pencil.
4. **Part 3 · About me** — two sentence starters about the child's pet/hero/kingdom.
5. **Check** — answers reveal one part at a time (never all at once).
6. **Print** — one A4 sheet + answer key on page 2; name and date line at the top.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- Writing on screen or on paper — both count; pencil is often better for letter work.
- Half-way brain break if the sheet takes more than 12 minutes.
- Never mark every letter; pick the one word to fix this week.
- The answer key is for the adult's eyes; fold it behind.
