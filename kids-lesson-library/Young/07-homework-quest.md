# 07 · Homework Quest

**Band:** Young (5–12) · **Type:** Activity (homework) · **Time:** 3 × 5 min across the week · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
Three short tasks over the week — one spoken, one written, one hunt or game — each naming the XP
it earns and how the child proves it was done. Between lessons is when language consolidates;
the quest format (XP, proof, one at a time) turns "homework" into three small wins. The child
can submit proof by tapping "done", a photo, or a voice note depending on the host.

## 2 · Goals
- The child **speaks English once at home** (a voice note or to a family member).
- The child **writes four sentences** using the week's language.
- The child **plays one language game** with a sibling, parent or the app.
- The tutor can see **which task was done** and praise it next lesson.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Your quest!" with the child's hero on the map.
2. **Task 1 · Speak** — record or tell someone; the quest gives the exact sentence starter.
3. **Task 2 · Write** — four sentences on paper or screen, with a word bank.
4. **Task 3 · Play** — a hunt or a mini-game; proof = a photo or a parent's word.
5. **Map** — three checkpoints light as tasks are proven; XP lands on the learner's real total.
6. Finish: "Quest complete!" + the family note for next week.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- Three tasks, five minutes each — cap it there; more homework kills motivation at this age.
- Every task must be doable without a device if the family prefers paper.
- Check the map next lesson and praise the *doing*, not the perfection.
- If the child only did one, that is a win; the map can finish later.
