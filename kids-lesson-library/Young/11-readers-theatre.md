# 11 · Reader's Theatre

**Band:** Young (6–12) · **Type:** Activity (reading fluency / performance) · **Time:** 20–30 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
A 10–14 line script for two or three roles, read aloud twice: once cold, once performed with a
voice for each character and one prop. Re-reading the same text for a performance is the classic
fluency method — speed and expression come from the second and third read, not from a new text —
and the audience (a parent, a toy, the camera) gives the reading a purpose. (Reader's theatre
fluency research; repeated-reading method.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **reads the script twice** (cold read + performance).
- The child **reads with expression** — shown by a voice change per character.
- The child **notices 2+ target words** inside the script and says them naturally.
- The tutor gets a recorded or live performance to praise specifically.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Cast it** — assign roles (child, tutor, or a toy takes a role).
3. **Cold read** — the script on screen, each line highlighted for its reader; tap = my line.
4. **Warm-up** — pick a "character voice" per role (squeaky, grand, sleepy).
5. **Performance** — no highlights; a "shine" button marks the best line.
6. **Curtain call** — applause sound; one line the child is proud of; family note to perform at home.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- The same script twice beats two scripts once.
- Never stop the performance for a correction; mark it and recast after the curtain.
- A toy or the camera is a legitimate audience for a shy reader.
- Keep each line under 12 words; bigger parts are not better parts.
