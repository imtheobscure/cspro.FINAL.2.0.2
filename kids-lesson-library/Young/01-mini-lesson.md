# 01 · Mini Lesson

**Band:** Young (5–12) · **Type:** Lesson (everyday core) · **Time:** 30–45 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
The everyday lesson, in five beats: a 60-second review of last session, 4–6 new words presented
with picture cues, a story or dialogue that uses them, a game with several fair turns, and a
two-minute wrap where the child says two sentences they could not say before. It is retrieval,
presentation, context, practice, production — the standard shape of an effective language
lesson, sized for one child. (Cambridge YLE lesson shape; Novakid/Outschool lesson structure.)

## 2 · Goals
- The child **retrieves last lesson's language** in the first two minutes (no prompts).
- The child **meets 4–6 new words** with a picture cue, then uses them in a story.
- The child **says two full sentences** using the new language at the end.
- The tutor has every line written out: present, drill, correct, praise.

## 3 · Interaction flow
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. **Fast review** — 3 quick questions from last session; stars for tries.
3. **New words** — one card at a time: picture, word, example sentence, listen and repeat.
4. **Story/dialogue** — the words inside one small scene (6–10 lines).
5. **The game** — 6–8 fair rounds using the words (quiz, sorting or matching).
6. **Wrap** — "two sentences you couldn't say before" + growth-mindset praise + family note.

## 4 · Tutor notes
- One structure or word-set per lesson; resist adding "just one more".
- The first two minutes are review, always — it is what makes the words stick.
- Let the child lose a turn but never the game; the win stays small and repeatable.
- 60-second brain break after every 5 minutes (stretch or a silly dance).
