# 14 · Big Quiz Show 🎮

**Band:** Young (6–12) · **Type:** Game (screen) · **Time:** 8–12 min · **Runtime:** one HTML file

## 1 · What it is
Six to ten questions with four big answers, read across a classroom: half on today's words, half
reviewing last lesson. Retrieval practice is the point — every question makes every child pull
something out of memory — and four options is the sweet spot: enough to think, few enough to read
from the back. (Kahoot-style quiz; Rosenshine retrieval.)

## 2 · Goals
- The whole room **retrieves 6+ items** in one sitting.
- The class **reviews last lesson** inside the opener rounds.
- The tutor sees **which items missed** (private tally) and replays them.
- Every child answers every round (all-hands up, not fastest-finger).

## 3 · How a round runs
1. Splash → "Let's go!".
2. A question fills the screen; four options, lettered A–D.
3. **Think time** — 5 silent seconds (a visible countdown that is gentle, not pressuring).
4. All hands up (or fingers 1–4), then the reveal; correct option blooms + a star.
5. Every 3rd question: "why?" — a modelled reason appears for the tutor to read.
6. After 6-10 rounds: class stars + a private "replay these two" list.

## 4 · Tutor & room notes
- Rotate who answers; the fastest hand never owns the game.
- Options within two words of each other in length; the correct one never in the same slot twice.
- If the room is guessing, drop one option for the next round.
- Points stay in the private tally; children only ever see stars.
